01 · Workstation

Everything a practice session needs, in one window.

The desktop studio is laid out like the hardware you already play: dockable panels, real faders, and nothing that gets between you and the keys.

Backing Track

Loop it. Slow it. Learn it.

Load an MP3 or WAV, set an A–B loop on the waveform, and drop section bookmarks at the playhead. Practice at half speed, then work back up to 1.5×.

  • A–B loop
  • 0.5–1.5× speed
  • Section bookmarks
  • Synced lyrics
Chord Intelligence

See the song's harmony.

Analysis detects the key, tempo and chord progression of your track. Chords show as names and Nashville numbers, with the next chord always previewed so you never get surprised.

  • Key + BPM detection
  • Nashville numbers
  • Next-chord preview
  • Learn Chords
Voicing Recommendations

Richer chords, one click away.

For every chord you get root position, inversions, 7th and add9 voicings on a mini keyboard. Hit Audition to hear it on your sound engine before you commit it to muscle memory.

  • Root · 1st Inv · 2nd Inv
  • 7th · Add9
  • Audition
Worship Drone Pad

A harmonic anchor under every song.

An ambient pad plays root, fifth and octave of the song key and crossfades smoothly when the key changes. It's the perfect bed for worship sets and for training your ear to the tonic.

  • Root + 5th + Octave
  • Smooth key crossfade
  • Remote toggle
Setlists & Scenes

Recall a whole sound in one tap.

Add songs to a setlist together with their patch snapshots. Save mixer states into eight scene presets and recall them from the desktop or the phone. Changes sync both ways.

  • Patch snapshots
  • Scene S1–S8
  • Desktop ⇄ phone sync
Mixer & Stage Warmth

Mix it like a real rig.

Four-channel output mixer with level meters, a 4-band master EQ with a live spectrum analyzer, and a Stage Warmth saturation stage with Tape and Crisp voicings.

  • Track · Synth · FX · Master
  • 4-band EQ + FFT
  • Tape / Crisp warmth
Key Shift

Transpose the backing track up or down 12 semitones to fit your voice or your band.

Metronome

Tap tempo, time signatures, and three click sounds: Studio Treble, Acoustic Woodblock and Digital Beep. One button syncs the grid to your track.

Input Telemetry

A live keyboard view with sustain, soft pedal, expression and volume meters, and a Learn Chords mode that records what you play.

Panic

One key (F12) kills stuck notes and resets controllers when a gig goes sideways.

02 · Chord intelligence

Think in numbers, play in any key.

Nashville numbers describe a progression by scale degree, so one chart works in every key. Litwave shows both the chord name and its number, in time with the track.

Pick a key and step through a classic worship progression to see how the display behaves in the app.

  • Current and next chord side by side
  • Four-bar harmonic measure grid
  • Slash chords and inversions shown correctly

03 · Sound lab

48 FX pads. A drone that never gets in the way.

Try the real layout right here. Six banks of eight MPC-style pads, plus the ambient drone. Turn your volume down a little, then tap or click.

FX Pads
Bank A · Percussion8 pads

Pads with a bundled preview play a recorded sample, the rest play a synthesized stand-in so you can feel the layout. The real app ships the full 48-sound library as 48 kHz stereo WAVs, and you can assign your own.

Ambient Drone Pad
Idle · choose a root

Root + fifth + octave. Tap another key to crossfade, tap the same key to release.

04 · Stage remote

Your phone is the music stand.

Open Litwave Remote on any phone or tablet on the same Wi-Fi. It's an installable web app (PWA) that mirrors the desktop in real time and puts the controls you need on stage under your thumb.

  1. 1
    Chords & PadHuge live chord with Nashville number, next-chord preview, song key and drone toggle.
  2. 2
    FX PadsTrigger the sampler with thumbs.
  3. 3
    Part 1–8 FadersBalance your sound parts from the stage.
  4. 4
    Song ManagerJump to any song in the setlist.
  5. 5
    MixerTrack, synth, FX and master levels.

Use the arrows on the phone to flip through the five stage pages.

05 · Ear training

Four drills that make the changes obvious.

Short, randomized exercises built into the app. Each one scores you as you go, so progress is something you can see.

Chord Quality

Hear a chord in a random key and name it: major, minor, 7th, and richer voicings.

C ? → Cmaj7
Progression

Hear a short progression in a given key and identify it by its Nashville numbers.

Key of G → 1 – 5 – 6 – 4
Melodic Response

Litwave plays a three or four note phrase. Play it back on your keyboard to score.

Listen → play it back
Relative Interval

Name the distance between two notes, from a minor 2nd upward.

C → E = Major 3rd
06 · Sound engines

Bring your Montage, or just bring your laptop.

Litwave hosts the instrument for you through native C++ engines, so your sound is always one launch away.

Yamaha MONTAGE MVST3

Host the Montage M plugin. Browse the voice catalog, balance parts, rename them, snapshot scenes and audition voicings straight from the desktop UI.

Community SoundFontNo plugin needed

No Montage? A built-in SoundFont engine gives you playable voices out of the box, with the same mixer, drone and FX pads on top.
